# 💳 Credit Card Financial Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes credit card transactions and customer information using SQL, Power BI, DAX, and CSV datasets. It provides interactive dashboards to monitor financial performance, identify customer spending patterns, and generate useful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze credit card revenue and weekly performance.
- Track transaction amounts, card categories, and expenditure types.
- Understand customer demographics based on age, income, gender, and state.
- Compare current-week and previous-week revenue.
- Monitor key metrics such as revenue, activation rate, and delinquency rate.
- Generate insights to support better business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **SQL:** Database creation, data storage, and data processing.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and visualization.
- **DAX:** Calculated columns, revenue calculations, and weekly comparisons.
- **Excel/CSV:** Source data preparation and data import.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Prepared customer and credit card transaction CSV files.
2. Created SQL tables and imported the datasets.
3. Connected SQL to Power BI and loaded the data.
4. Created calculated fields and measures using DAX.
5. Developed transaction and customer dashboards.
6. Analyzed performance trends and prepared business recommendations.

## 📊 Dashboard Reports

### 1. Credit Card Transaction Report
- Quarterly revenue analysis.
- Revenue by card category.
- Spending analysis by expenditure type.
- Weekly revenue performance.

### 2. Credit Card Customer Report
- Customer age and income group analysis.
- State-wise revenue analysis.
- Gender-based revenue comparison.
- Customer segmentation and behavior analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Year-to-date revenue was reported as 57M.
- Revenue increased by 28.8% compared with the previous week.
- Blue and Silver cards accounted for approximately 93% of overall transactions.
- Texas, New York, and California contributed approximately 68% of revenue.
- The customer activation rate was 57.5%.
- The delinquency rate was 6.06%.

*Note: These insights are based on the dashboard report for Week 53.*

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Improve card activation through customer onboarding and reminders.
- Maintain strong engagement among Blue and Silver card users.
- Monitor delinquency trends across major contributing states.
- Explore opportunities to improve revenue from female customers.
